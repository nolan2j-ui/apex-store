Returns, exchanges & shipping

Wrong size? Changed your mind? We keep this simple.

30 days, no cost to you

  • You have 30 days from the day your order arrives to send it back.
  • Size exchanges are free. So are returns: we send you a prepaid return label, and there is no restocking fee.
  • Items should be unworn and unwashed, with tags on.
  • Returns are by mail and this policy covers orders shipped within the United States.

How to start a return or exchange

  1. Email apexhybridco@gmail.com with your order number, the item, and the size you want (or say you want a refund).
  2. We reply with a prepaid label, usually within one business day.
  3. Drop the package off. We ship your new size as soon as the return is scanned, or refund your original payment method once it reaches us.

Shipping

Shipping is free on every US order. Orders are made and shipped from our US fulfillment partners, and you get a tracking link by email as soon as your order leaves.
