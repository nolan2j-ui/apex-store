1. Sled Push: Stacking Skeletal Alignment

Lock your elbows or tuck the poles tight into your anterior deltoids at a 45-degree shin angle so force transfers straight through your hips rather than burning out your triceps. Train in the Sled Push Dept. Muscle Tank (APC-C29) or CoolCore™ Turf Tech Shirt (APC-C40) to keep core temperature low.

2. Sled Pull: Hip-Hinge Walking vs. Arm Pulling

Never pull the sled rope with biceps alone. Apply ChalkLock Liquid Lifting Chalk (APC-E23) before your wave, clamp the rope with 2-inch-trained grip strength built on DeadHang Grip Handles (APC-E27), and initiate every pull by driving your heels back to the rear line of the 1.8m box.
