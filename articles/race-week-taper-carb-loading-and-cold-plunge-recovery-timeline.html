The 7-Day Race Week Taper, Electrolyte Loading & Post-Race Cold Plunge Timeline

Day-by-day taper volume cuts, sodium/carbohydrate targets, pre-race warmup priming, and the 48-hour post-race cold plunge + pneumatic compression recovery timeline.

You cannot build new aerobic fitness in the final 7 days before race day—you can only shed accumulated central nervous system fatigue and top off intramuscular glycogen and plasma volume.

1. Days -7 to -2: Volume Reduction & Sodium Priming

Cut running and station volume by 50–60% while keeping 3–4 short 200m race-pace strides to maintain neuromuscular tension. Mix 1,000mg sodium electrolyte servings inside your 24oz Insulated Stainless Shaker Bottle (APC-C15) across the 36 hours prior to wave start.

2. Hours +1 to +48 Post-Race: The Contrast & Compression Flush

Once you cross the finish arch, eccentric muscle damage peaks between 12 and 36 hours. Start with a 3-minute 50°F immersion in the CryoPod 105-Gallon Cold Plunge Tub (APC-E03) to blunt acute inflammation, followed that evening by 20 minutes in AirRecover Pneumatic Compression Boots (APC-E12) and targeted quad work with the ApexPulse Pro 3200 RPM Massage Gun (APC-E01).
