Weighted Training Vest 20lb Protocol: Murph Prep, Rucking & Compromised Running Intervals

How to program a 20-lb plate-carrier weighted training vest and chest-strap heart rate monitor to build Zone-2 aerobic density and leg durability without joint overload.

Whether you are preparing for Memorial Day Murph, building eccentric quad stamina for downhill running, or stacking Zone-2 rucking volume, a balanced Weighted Training Vest 20lb changes the biomechanical demand of every step and bodyweight rep.

1. Why Fit & Weight Distribution Matter on the Run

Traditional sand-bag vests sag against the lower ribs and bounce violently during running intervals. The TactFit Weighted Training Vest 20lb uses an ergonomic X-frame harness with dual chest and waist tension straps that lock the load high and tight against your center of mass—allowing full thoracic expansion during 1-mile bookend runs and pull-up sets.

2. Tracking True Zone-2 Heart Rate Under Load

Adding 20 lbs of external load elevates cardiac output by 12–18 BPM at any given pace. Because wrist-based optical sensors suffer from cadence lock when your arms swing or grip pull-up bars, pair your vest sessions with the PulseTrack ANT+ & Bluetooth 5.0 Chest Strap Heart Rate Monitor to keep your aerobic rucking strictly below your ventilatory threshold (VT1).

3. Post-Vest Patellar & Joint Decompression

Weighted squats, lunges, and running increase patellofemoral tendon load. Immediately after stripping off your vest and Run. Station. Repeat. Functional Race Tee, apply 15 minutes of 360-degree cold compression with the CryoSleeve Knee Ice Pack Wrap Cold Compression to calm acute synovial inflammation.
