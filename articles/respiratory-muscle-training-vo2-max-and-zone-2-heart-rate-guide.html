Every hybrid racer knows the sensation of exiting the 1,000-meter SkiErg or burpee broad jump station: your lungs burn, your breathing becomes shallow, and your legs suddenly feel like concrete. That phenomenon has a physiological name—the respiratory metaboreflex.

1. How Diaphragm Fatigue Steals Leg Blood Flow

When your inspiratory and expiratory muscles fatigue under sustained Zone-4 ventilation, your sympathetic nervous system triggers vasoconstriction in your limbs, diverting oxygenated blood away from your quads to keep your diaphragm contracting. Training with the AeroLung Breathing Lung Trainer twice daily (30 breaths morning and night) hypertrophies the diaphragm and intercostals so they resist fatigue at high minute ventilation.

2. Pacing Threshold Repeats with ECG-Accurate Telemetry
