The Complete 8-Station Functional Race Day & Recovery Checklist

From breathable 4.2 oz race tees and ventilated shoe duffles to deep-tissue percussion massagers and pneumatic compression boots—how to pack and recover for race weekend.

Preparing for an 8-station functional endurance race requires training both your aerobic threshold and your compromised muscular stamina. Just as critical is the gear you wear across eight 1,000-meter runs and the recovery hardware you use in the 72 hours after crossing the finish line.

1. Breathable Race Tees vs. Heavyweight Warmup Layers

During a 60-to-90-minute indoor functional race, heat dissipation determines how well you hold your Zone 4 pace after heavy sled pushes and wall balls. Our Run. Station. Repeat. Functional Race Tee uses 4.2 oz Airlume combed ring-spun cotton that breathes through high-output stations without clinging, while our 8.0 oz Fleece Warmup Hoodie keeps your core warm in the athlete corral before wave start.

2. Compartment Organization in the Athlete Corral

Nothing spikes pre-race stress faster than digging through a disorganized bag for electrolytes, chalk, or a spare pair of racing shoes. The 8-Station Race Day 50L All-Weather Gym Duffle separates damp warmup gear from race essentials with a dedicated ventilated shoe tunnel.

3. Post-Race Myofascial & Lymphatic Flushing

Within two hours of finishing 100 wall balls and heavy sled pulls, eccentric micro-trauma peaks in the quads, calves, and lower back. Pairing a 12mm-amplitude ApexPulse Pro 3200 RPM Percussion Massager with 20 minutes in AirRecover Pneumatic Compression Boots accelerates venous return and cuts delayed-onset muscle soreness in half.
