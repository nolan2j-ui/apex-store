Heavyweight Pump Covers vs. Race-Weight Tees: How to Layer for Training

Why 6.5 oz carded cotton oversized tees and 8.0 oz fleece hoodies are the warmup standard before stripping down to 4.2 oz race-weight cotton.

Walk into any serious functional strength room from Southern California to Austin and you will notice a pattern: athletes warm up in structured, heavyweight cotton pump covers before stripping down to breathable race-weight tees once heart rates cross 160 BPM.

1. What Makes a True Pump Cover?

A proper warmup tee needs enough fabric weight (6.0 to 6.5 oz/yd²) to drape cleanly over the shoulders without clinging during barbell warmups. Our Wall Balls & Bad Decisions Heavyweight Pump Cover is cut with a relaxed drop-shoulder silhouette that holds its structure wash after wash.

2. Breathable 4.2 oz Cotton for High-Output Stations

Once you transition to 1,000-meter runs, burpee broad jumps, and erg intervals, fabric weight matters. Our Sled Push Enthusiast* and Sled Push Dept. tees use 4.2 oz Airlume combed ring-spun cotton printed with breathable water-based inks in the USA.
