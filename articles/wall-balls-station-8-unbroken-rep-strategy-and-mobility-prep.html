Station 08 Is Where Podiums and Sub-65 Qualifiers Are Decided

Arriving at the Station 08 Wall Ball rig after 8,000 meters of running and 100 meters of sandbag lunges is the ultimate test of neuromuscular efficiency. A single no-rep for shallow squat depth or a low target strike wastes precious energy when your anterior deltoids and quads are already redlined.

1. Fix Your Squat Depth Before Race Week: Ankle Dorsiflexion & Thoracic Extension

When ankle dorsiflexion is restricted, your heels lift or your torso pitches forward at the bottom of the squat—forcing your lower back and shoulders to heave the 6kg/9kg ball from a terrible angle. Spend 5 minutes before every lower-body session on the Tibialis Anterior Trainer Bar (APC-E10) and mobilize your upper back on the VibeRoll Pro 4-Speed Vibrating Foam Roller (APC-E04) so you can catch the ball in a stacked front-rack position.

2. Overhead Freedom: Eliminate Shoulder Pinch on Reps 60–100

Standard cotton sleeves bind across the wet deltoid after 60 minutes of sweating, adding subtle resistance on every overhead throw. That is why elite racers compete in the deep-cut 100 Wall Balls Unbroken Sleeveless Muscle Tank (APC-C31) or warm up in the 100 Wall Balls Unbroken* Heavyweight Tee (APC-C03) and Wall Balls & Bad Decisions Heavyweight Hoodie (APC-C10).

3. The Championship 100-Rep Pacing Blueprint

  • Pro / Sub-60 Division (2–3 Sets): 45 – 30 – 25 reps with a strict 5-second hands-on-rig breath reset.
  • Competitive Open Division (5 Sets): 25 – 20 – 20 – 20 – 15 reps. Let your arms drop completely as the ball travels upward to flush shoulder capillaries between every catch.
  • Post-Finisher Patellar Care: Immediately after crossing the finisher arch, wrap both knees in the CryoSleeve 360° Gel Compression Knee Ice Wrap (APC-E15) to calm patellar tendon inflammation.
