53% of Your Total Race Time Happens on the 1,000m Track

In an 8-station hybrid race, the eight 1,000m runs account for more than half of your total clock time—yet most athletes train stations in isolation and treat the runs as recovery jogs. When you exit Station 02 (50m Heavy Sled Push) and Station 03 (50m Sled Pull), intramuscular pressure and localized quad acidosis alter your ground-contact mechanics. Mastering compromised running is the fastest way to drop 4+ minutes off your personal best.

The 8×1,000m Split Pacing Matrix

  • Run 01 (Pre-SkiErg): Run at Target Pace + 3 seconds. Stay capped below 86% max HR on your PulsePro ANT+ & Bluetooth Chest-Strap Heart Rate Monitor (APC-E18).
  • Run 02 (Post-SkiErg): Settle directly into Target Pace. Upper-body lats and triceps are taxed, so relax your shoulders in the Dry-Pace™ 1KM Split Moisture-Wicking Tech Tee (APC-C37).

  • Run 05 (Post-80m Burpee Broad Jumps): Heart rate peaks here. Focus on nasal-assisted exhalations and ultra-light heat dissipation in the Featherweight 3.8 oz Sub-60 Hybrid Race Tee (APC-C39).
