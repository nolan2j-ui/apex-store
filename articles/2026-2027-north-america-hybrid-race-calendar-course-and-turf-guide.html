2026–2027 North America Functional Race Calendar: Dallas, Chicago, Anaheim & Miami Turf Pacing Guide

Complete venue breakdown, carpet friction notes, Roxzone transition layouts, and station pacing strategies for upcoming 2026/2027 North American and World Championship race weekends.

Not all 8-station indoor race courses run at the same speed. Between high-friction convention center sled carpets, multi-lap perimeter run tracks, and coastal humidity, understanding your venue's specific layout can save 2 to 4 minutes off your finish time.

1. Chicago (Navy Pier) & Dallas (Kay Bailey Hutchison Convention Center)

Chicago's long exhibition hall layout rewards disciplined 1,000m pacing on lap one, while Dallas features dense sled turf that demands low-handle drive angle and rigid carbon-plate or high-traction outsoles. Gear up with the Chicago Championship Division Tee (APC-C16), Dallas Lone Star Tee (APC-C17), and the 2026/2027 World Series Prep Hoodie (APC-C34).

2. Anaheim / SoCal, Las Vegas & Miami Beach

Southern California and Las Vegas offer fast, dry indoor air ideal for sub-65 PR attempts, whereas Miami's high dew point requires aggressive pre-race sodium loading and ultra-breathable AeroMesh™ Tech Shirts to prevent core overheating before Station 7 lunges.

3. Packing for Away-Race Weekends

Never check your race shoes. Pack your carbon racers in the SoleVault Ventilated Shoe Case (APC-E26) and keep a MorphRoll Collapsible Travel Foam Roller (APC-E25) in your carry-on for hotel-room quad flushing the night before wave start.
